Can I Trade E-Mini Russell 2000 on a $25K TPT Account?

On a $25K TPT evaluation account, RTY (E-Mini Russell 2000) is tradeable under the firm's standard rules. The starting balance is $25,000 against a profit target of $1,500. The daily loss limit is $1,000, which works out to 20.0 RTY points before the day's trading is cut off — a hard number worth knowing before sizing a position, not an estimate. The end-of-day trailing drawdown floor is $1,500, measured from the account's high-water mark, not its starting balance — the detail most sizing guides under-explain and the one that actually determines how much room there is left on a losing streak. The account's own rules allow up to 3 contracts of RTY at once; the commonly used 25% sizing rule brings that down to 1 contract(s) as a day-to-day working size, trading some upside for a real cushion against a single bad day wiping out the daily loss limit. The one-time evaluation fee for this tier is $150.
