Can I Trade Micro E-Mini S&P 500 on a $75K TPT Account?

On a $75K TPT evaluation account, MES (Micro E-Mini S&P 500) is tradeable under the firm's standard rules. The starting balance is $75,000 against a profit target of $4,250. The daily loss limit is $2,250, which works out to 450.0 MES points before the day's trading is cut off — a hard number worth knowing before sizing a position, not an estimate. The end-of-day trailing drawdown floor is $2,750, measured from the account's high-water mark, not its starting balance — the detail most sizing guides under-explain and the one that actually determines how much room there is left on a losing streak. The account's own rules allow up to 9 contracts of MES at once; the commonly used 25% sizing rule brings that down to 2 contract(s) as a day-to-day working size, trading some upside for a real cushion against a single bad day wiping out the daily loss limit. The one-time evaluation fee for this tier is $245.
