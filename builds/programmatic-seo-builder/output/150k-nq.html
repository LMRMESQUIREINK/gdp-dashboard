Can I Trade E-Mini NASDAQ-100 on a $150K TPT Account?

On a $150K TPT evaluation account, NQ (E-Mini NASDAQ-100) is tradeable under the firm's standard rules. The starting balance is $150,000 against a profit target of $9,000. The daily loss limit is $3,300, which works out to 165.0 NQ points before the day's trading is cut off — a hard number worth knowing before sizing a position, not an estimate. The end-of-day trailing drawdown floor is $4,500, measured from the account's high-water mark, not its starting balance — the detail most sizing guides under-explain and the one that actually determines how much room there is left on a losing streak. The account's own rules allow up to 15 contracts of NQ at once; the commonly used 25% sizing rule brings that down to 3 contract(s) as a day-to-day working size, trading some upside for a real cushion against a single bad day wiping out the daily loss limit. The one-time evaluation fee for this tier is $360.
