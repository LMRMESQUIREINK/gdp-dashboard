Can I Trade E-Mini Russell 2000 on a $50K TPT Account?

On a $50K TPT evaluation account, RTY (E-Mini Russell 2000) is tradeable under the firm's standard rules. The starting balance is $50,000 against a profit target of $3,000. The daily loss limit is $1,100, which works out to 22.0 RTY points before the day's trading is cut off — a hard number worth knowing before sizing a position, not an estimate. The end-of-day trailing drawdown floor is $2,000, measured from the account's high-water mark, not its starting balance — the detail most sizing guides under-explain and the one that actually determines how much room there is left on a losing streak. The account's own rules allow up to 6 contracts of RTY at once; the commonly used 25% sizing rule brings that down to 1 contract(s) as a day-to-day working size, trading some upside for a real cushion against a single bad day wiping out the daily loss limit. The one-time evaluation fee for this tier is $170.
