# Discovery call notes (SYNTHETIC — demonstration only, not a real client)

Written as rough notes from a discovery call, to demonstrate the
`proposal-generator` skill end-to-end. No real client, company, or deal
is represented here.

- Client: Mara, runs a small D2C skincare brand ("Thistle & Oat"),
  ~$40K/month revenue, currently on Shopify.
- Pain: checkout abandonment is high — she thinks it's because shipping
  cost only shows up at the very last step. No analytics confirming
  this yet, just her instinct from watching session recordings.
- Wants: shipping cost estimate shown earlier in the flow (product page
  or cart), and a cleaner mobile checkout — says "most of my traffic is
  Instagram, so it's all mobile."
- Explicitly NOT asking for: a full site redesign, new branding, or a
  switch off Shopify. Said this twice, seemed burned by a past agency
  that scope-crept a "small fix" into a full rebuild.
- Budget: mentioned "somewhere under $5K" but didn't commit to a number.
- Deadline: wants it live before a planned influencer push in 5 weeks.
- Asked directly: "what happens if this takes longer than you think."
