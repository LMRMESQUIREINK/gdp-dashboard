FAQ

Straight answers

Do I stop using my Oura/Whoop app?

No. Recovery Desk reads the same data your ring or strap already collects — it doesn't replace the app, it replaces the part where you have to open it and interpret it yourself.

What if the text says to see a doctor?

That only happens when the overnight pattern looks more like illness than training fatigue — depressed HRV together with an elevated resting heart rate or breathing rate and poor sleep, not any one signal alone. On those mornings we never recommend training, full stop.

Is this medical advice?

No. Recovery Desk is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything — every message says so. It's a training-decision tool built on your own wearable data.

Which wearables work?

Oura and Whoop at launch.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — text STOP, any time, no phone call, no retention flow.
